Sqft pace (Standard): ✓ Real data (July 2026) — 29 logged recurring light-condition jobs, team of 2 (1 outlier excluded — very light scope). Formula: 1.14 hrs base + 0.63 hrs per 1,000 sqft.
Client rate: ✓ Decided (July 2026) — $150/hr team ($75/tech), chosen after comparing margin at $130.90/hr vs $150/hr against real overhead. Applies to Standard & A la carte.
Frequency discount (Standard): ✓ Decided (July 2026) — Weekly -15%, Bi-Weekly -10%, Tri-Weekly -5%, Monthly & One-Time at base rate. Business decision — real CRM job-time data was checked but too inconsistent to derive an exact number.
Hours (specialty services): ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — Deep Clean and Move-In/Out use real median job time (105 completed jobs, 2 clock-error entries excluded): 4.88hrs each, heavy-condition-anchored, scaled by the light/moderate/heavy selector. Post Construction uses an owner estimate instead — the real 14-job sample (3.91hrs) was thin and contradicted known job difficulty, so it's set at 5hrs (team of 2) or 4hrs (team of 3, owner's estimate of speed-up), with a crew-size selector now on that service. Sqft was dropped as an hours driver across all three — real data showed no reliable sqft/time link.
Pricing model (specialty services): ✓ Changed (July 2026) — old historical $ price bands replaced with hours × team rate, shown as a ±20% quote range (e.g. "$585–$879") to give clients a digestible range while covering unpredictable buildup. Internal cost/margin math uses the expected (point-estimate) hours and price, not the high end of the range.
First-time client discount: ✓ Added (July 2026) — competitor research showed real market pricing for a 3BR one-time light-condition Standard Clean sits at the very top of the local range ($341). A 20% first-clean discount (inspired by competitor SoCal Cleaners' identical offer) softens this at the moment that matters most for conversion — a simple checkbox, applies across all services, shown transparently in the internal margin math.
Add-on fees/time: ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — linens $10/+15min, baseboards $25/+30min (free-included in Deep Clean, Move In/Out, Post Construction), laundry $50/+30min (up to 2 loads), fridge interior $80/+1hr, oven interior $50/+45min, sliding glass doors $5/+1min each. Microwave interior is free/no time in every service — not a chargeable add-on.
Payroll tax: ✓ Confirmed — 10.5% of wages, per your confirmation.
Card processing fee: ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — the old undefined "10% admin fee" is now the real Stripe rate: 2.9% + $0.30/transaction. The office assistant's cost lives in the Overhead line above, not here, to avoid double-counting.
City / hot-zone pricing: ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — real median household income (2024 Census/ACS) pulled for all 7 hot zones plus 9 other Southern OC cities, benchmarked against the OC median ($116,300). Tiered premium (business decision, owner-confirmed): Corona Del Mar & Rancho Santa Margarita +5%, Laguna Beach/Laguna Niguel/Irvine +3%, Huntington Beach & Costa Mesa base rate. All other Southern OC cities are base rate regardless of income — Tustin is the one city that came in notably below the OC median (-13%), flagged for reference only, no automatic discount applied.
Housekeeping mileage: ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — real payroll mileage log matched to Jo Maceda's home (office ↔ client, 20.31mi one-way, logged from old office). The old and new office are only ~6 miles apart, so the real distance once the Brookhollow Dr move is complete should land close to this — worth a quick recheck after the move, but not expected to shift much. Round-trip mileage + 1hr drive-time wage @ CA minimum wage ($16.90/hr) now built into the Housekeeping cost ledger.
Airbnb pricing: ✓ Resolved (July 2026) — replaced the $180 flat-rate placeholder with real sqft-based hours (same pace formula as Standard Clean), plus ~12 min bundled for fridge/oven/linens/laundry (owner estimate — done in parallel, minimal extra time since turnovers rarely have heavy buildup). Laundry beyond 2 loads is +$10/load. Volume discount for frequent turnovers: 2x/week -5%, 3x/week -10%.
Overhead per hour: ✓ Added (July 2026) — supplies, insurance, software, and office staff ($4,452.60/mo) divided by that month's team-hours. Auto-selects by calendar month. Jan, Feb, Mar, Jun, Jul, Nov, Dec use real TCS hours; Apr, May, Aug, Sep, Oct are estimated by scaling 2025 hours up by the observed year-over-year growth rate (1.71x) — flagged in the ledger when estimated.
