Management-only view behind the Route Day Planner used by Sales — arrival-window profitability and the weekly route by city, including bookings logged since the baseline was pulled.

Arrival window profitability

Average price, margin %, and revenue/hour by arrival window from completed jobs. 10AM–1PM stands out across nearly every service type — not just one account — so it's used as the default suggestion in the Route Day Planner. Two raw time-slot labels from the source data are left out: one was a single commercial account with an implausibly low logged time (a data quirk, not real work), and one was entirely $0 walk-through estimates, not paid cleanings.

Completed jobs by city and weekday, plus anything logged since (manually, or automatically when a quote is marked Won). Darker cells are a city's established day — that's where a new lead nearby costs the least to add.
